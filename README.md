# World Flags

Every country's flag, one drag away from your Drawdy board. Search by name, code or nickname, pick round, rectangle or square, and each flag lands with the country's name underneath.

## What it's for

- **Geography and language lessons**: maps, quizzes and "where is it?" games with real flags.
- **Travel plans**: mark the countries on a trip, a route or a bucket list.
- **Global teams**: show where everyone works, time zones, offices and markets.
- **Sports and events**: match brackets, predictions and medal tables.

## How to use

1. Click the **World Flags** button on the right of the board.
2. Pick a region: **All**, **Africa**, **Americas**, **Asia**, **Europe**, **Oceania** or **More** (the European Union, the United Nations and Antarctica). Or type in the search box; press `/` to jump to it.
3. Search by country name (Japan), code (JP) or a common nickname (UK, USA, Holland, Ivory Coast).
4. Drag a flag onto the board and let go where you want it. Or click it to add it in the middle of your view; more clicks line flags up in rows instead of stacking them.
5. In the search box, press Enter to add the top result.

## Options

- **Size**: S, M, L or XL, from 32 to 256 board units.
- **Style**: **Round** badges, **Rectangle** flags in the usual 4:3 shape, or **Square** tiles.
- **Label**: the country's name, centred under the flag. On by default, and the flag and its label are grouped so they move together.

## Tips

- England, Scotland, Wales, Northern Ireland, Catalonia, Galicia and the Basque Country have their own flags too.
- Your last 12 flags stay in a **Recent** row and switch style with the rest of the palette. The palette remembers your options.
- Flags are vector images, so they stay sharp however far you zoom in.

## Privacy

- The flags download from the Iconify API as you scroll. Nothing you draw or type is sent anywhere.
- Your recent flags and options are saved in this browser, so they are the same on every board you open here.
