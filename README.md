# World Flags ✈(◕‿◕✿)

**every flag, labelled, one drag away.**

265 country and region flags, round, rectangle or square. Each one lands with the country's name underneath, so maps, trips and team boards explain themselves ✧

![Searching World Flags for Japan, Brazil and Kenya, switching to rectangle flags for the UK, and dragging each labelled flag onto a Drawdy board](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/mickyngub/drawdy-world-flags/main/docs/media/world-flags.gif)

---

## let's travel ٩(◕‿◕)۶

1. Click the **globe** in the extension rail.
2. Search by name, code or nickname, like *Japan*, *JP* or *UK*, or pick a region: Africa, Americas, Asia, Europe, Oceania or More. Press `/` to jump to search.
3. Drag a flag onto the board. Or click it to add it in the middle of your view; more clicks line up in a row.
4. Press **Enter** in the search box to add the top result.

> (・_・;) **flags still loading?** They download as you scroll, so give a slow connection a second.

---

## make it yours (｡•̀ᴗ-)✧

- **Round**, **Rectangle** or **Square**: badges, classic 4:3 flags, or tiles.
- **Label**: the country's name underneath, on by default and grouped with the flag.
- **Size**: S, M, L or XL.

---

## little things to try (っ˘ω˘ς )

- A "where is it?" geography quiz: flags on one side, a map on the other.
- Your trip, country by country, with arrows in between.
- Where your team works, one round flag per person.
- World Cup predictions, with England, Scotland and Wales included.

---

**the small print**: flags download from the Iconify API as you scroll; nothing you draw or type leaves your board. Your recent flags and options stay in this browser. Round flags from [circle-flags](https://github.com/HatScripts/circle-flags) (MIT), rectangle and square flags from [flag-icons](https://github.com/lipis/flag-icons) (MIT).
